Recordings are saved in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back later on this same device. When you're done, download them and upload the folder to your website.

Keyboard: Space starts and stops recording · P plays · ← and → change words. Recording stops on its own after 4 seconds.
    How to put the recordings on your website
    1. Click Download recordings (.zip) and unzip the file. Inside is a folder named audio. With more than 90 recordings you'll instead see folders named upload 1 of 4, upload 2 of 4 and so on, each holding an audio folder, because GitHub's website accepts at most 100 files per upload.
    2. Open your repository on GitHub and click Add file → Upload files.
    3. Drag the audio folder in and click Commit changes. If you have several upload folders, repeat this for the audio folder inside each one; they all merge into the same folder on your site.
    4. After a minute or two, every device uses your recordings. Words marked website are already live.

    Re-recorded a few words? Download again and upload; files with the same name are replaced.
